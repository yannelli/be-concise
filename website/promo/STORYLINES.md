# Concise promo: storylines

Picked: **D** (the person asked for something dramatic and cinematic, with kinetic motion and depth).
A, B and C stay below so a later turn can switch.

The one thing: your coding agent's words get checked before they land, so you read less.
Viewer task: a developer whose agent is writing a change to `src/auth.ts`, a commit, and a PR.
Real data that travels: the 5-line comment from the homepage demo, a commit message the
vocabulary check really flags ("leverage", "robust", "seamless", "streamline"), and the
console's Live activity record of those denials. Every hook message on screen is real hook output.

## A. One diff, start to finish (recommended)

Device: life before and after, shown as the same shot improved. Form: one continuous take.
The camera never cuts. It travels along a single change as the agent writes it. Each time the
agent pads something, the real Concise message arrives from the edge and the text shrinks in
place. At the end the camera pulls back and the whole change was being recorded in the web console.

- First 2 s: frame 0 is a code pane, already mid-write, with a 5-line comment ("// Check whether
  the provided user has / permission to access…") filling half the frame and its lines still arriving.
- Beats: hook (comment piles up) → tension (it keeps going, line 5) → reveal ("[concise] Comment is
  5 lines (limit 2)" slides in; the word Concise lands) → proof (comment collapses to 1 line; the
  camera pans right to a commit message, "leverage / robust / seamless" underline in orange, real
  [concise:vocabulary] denial, rewrite to one plain line; pan down to a PR body that
  folds from an essay to one paragraph) → payoff (pull back: it's the console's Live activity page,
  each check listed) → claim "Your agent. Less verbose." → end card (mark, wordmark, install command).
- Look: the site's own warm-grey ground (#F6F6F3), ink #18191D, orange #FF4A24 only on what
  Concise flags and on the end card; Jost for titles, the code at 1.8x real size.
- Sound: a sparse, dry pulse (~100 bpm). Soft key ticks while the agent writes. A muted thunk on
  each denial. The music opens up on the pull-back to the console.

## B. The wall of agent-speak (the bold one)

Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
Real phrases from Concise's 47 writing categories ("It is important to note that", "delve",
"leverage", "In conclusion", "I hope this helps!") stack up on the beat until they fill the frame
edge to edge. One orange stroke cuts through the wall and it falls away, leaving one short line.
Then three hard cuts, one per limit, each made physical, and the console closes it.

- First 2 s: "It is important to note that" fills the frame at 200 px; "delve" and "leverage" slam
  in on the next two beats, crowding it.
- Beats: hook (phrases pile up) → tension (the wall fills, text too dense to read) → reveal (the
  stroke, "Concise") → proof (a comment block squeezed to 2 lines; a file scroll stopped at line
  300; a PR body trimmed to 1 paragraph, 3 sentences) → payoff (the console Overview with its real
  counts) → claim → end card.
- Look: full-bleed ink and orange colour fields swapping every bar, Jost at display sizes, no UI
  until the console.
- Sound: a driving 120 bpm beat. Cuts land on the bar. The music drops out for one beat at the stroke.

## C. Review at 6 pm

Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: split screen to one.
A reviewer opens their agent's PR at the end of the day. Left: without Concise, the PR body
scrolls and scrolls, comments everywhere, the scroll bar shrinking. Right: the same change with
Concise. The right side plays calmly while the left churns, then the left slides away and the
camera pushes into the right side's console.

- First 2 s: a clock reading 6:04 pm and a GitHub-style notification "Your agent opened a pull request".
- Beats: hook (the notification) → tension (left pane: the essay keeps scrolling) → reveal (right
  pane: the same PR, one paragraph; "Concise" named) → proof (the comment, the commit, the test
  run each shown side by side) → payoff (left collapses; push into the console) → claim → end card.
- Look: two panes on the warm-grey ground, the left slightly desaturated, the right full colour.
- Sound: a calm bed under the right pane and a busy tick under the left that cuts out when the left
  collapses.

## Assumptions

28–32 s, 1920 × 1080 at 30 fps, no voiceover. Music bed and a few effects if a music source is
available here; if not, I deliver silent and say how to add a track. On the homepage it sits
under the hero as a click-to-play video with sound, with frame 0 as the poster. Rendered to
MP4 (H.264) plus a WebM, both kept under about 6 MB.

## D. The wall, in depth (built)

Device: type carries the argument, then the feature as proof. Form: a flight through one world.
B's wall of agent-speak, rebuilt as a 3D space the camera flies through. Real phrases from
Concise's pattern references rush past in depth until they stack into a wall; one orange stroke
splits it; the camera then flies between three stations (a comment, a commit, a PR) where the
real hook messages land and the text shrinks; it pulls back into the real web console and ends
on the mark.

- First 2 s: the camera already moving forward through "It is important to note that",
  "delve", "leverage", "I hope this helps!" at several depths, near ones blurred.
- Beats: hook (flight) → tension (the wall slams together, beat by beat; the music stops) →
  reveal (orange stroke, the wall splits, "Concise checks it first") → proof (comment, commit,
  PR, each with its real denial) → payoff (the console, real screenshots) → claim → end card.
- Look: ink #18191D for the noise, a colour flood to the site's #F7F7F5 at the stroke, orange
  #FF4A24 only on what Concise flags, the stroke and the mark. Jost 550.
- Sound: ElevenLabs music_v2_5, 120 bpm, a build that cuts dead on the last beat before the stroke,
  a drop on the stroke, a peak on the console, a ringing chord under the end card.
