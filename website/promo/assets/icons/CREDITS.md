The original files are kept here as vendored sources. Their unmodified path/circle/line geometry is embedded in `compositions/proof.html`; only presentation and accessibility attributes are added. Inline `currentColor` inherits the existing brand ink rather than introducing a new colour. The user's requested faint treatment uses 8% opacity. The three SVGs are decorative, hidden from accessibility APIs, and excluded individually from layout auditing because their overlap with the titles is intentional. Text remains audited and paints above them.
